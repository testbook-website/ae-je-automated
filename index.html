Energy vs Exergy

Energy is conserved (1st law). Exergy is the part of energy that can be converted to useful work against a given environment, and it is destroyed by every irreversibility (2nd law).

Formula sheet

Subscript 0 = dead state (environment). Units: kJ, kJ/kg, K.

Exergy of heat
XQ = Q · (1 − T₀/T)
(1 − T₀/T) is the Carnot factor.
Closed system (non-flow) exergy
φ = (u−u₀) + P₀(v−v₀) − T₀(s−s₀) + V²/2 + gz
Specific, kJ/kg.
Flow exergy (stream)
ψ = (h−h₀) − T₀(s−s₀) + V²/2 + gz
For control volumes / steady flow.
Ideal gas flow exergy
ψ = cp(T−T₀) − T₀[cp ln(T/T₀) − R ln(P/P₀)]
Air: cp = 1.005, R = 0.287 kJ/kg·K.
Gouy–Stodola theorem
I = Xdestroyed = T₀ · Sgen
I = irreversibility.
Irreversibility (work form)
I = Wrev − Wuseful
For work-producing devices.
Second law efficiency
ηII = ηth / ηrev = Wu / Wrev
Heat engines.
Second law efficiency (refrigerator / heat pump)
ηII = COP / COPrev
COPHP,rev = TH/(TH − TL)

Solved numerical

Air flows at 500 K and 1 bar. Dead state: 300 K, 1 bar. Find the flow exergy and the fraction of enthalpy that is exergy. Take cp = 1.005 kJ/kg·K.

1
Pressure term vanishes since P = P₀: R ln(P/P₀) = 0
2
Enthalpy above dead state: cₚ(T−T₀) = 1.005 × 200 = 201.00 kJ/kg
3
Entropy term: T₀·cₚ·ln(T/T₀) = 300 × 1.005 × ln(1.6667) = 301.5 × 0.5108 = 154.01 kJ/kg
4
Flow exergy: ψ = 201.00 − 154.01 = 46.99 kJ/kg
5
Exergy fraction: 46.99 / 201.00 = 23.4%
ψ ≈ 47.0 kJ/kg. Only 23.4% of the stream's enthalpy can be converted to work.
